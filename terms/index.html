Services

Property Flash provides property photography, floor plan production, drone photography, walkthrough video, and EPC assessment services to estate agents and property owners across West Yorkshire.

Bookings are confirmed once you receive a calendar confirmation from Cal.com. We reserve the right to refuse or reschedule a booking with reasonable notice.

Cancellations & rescheduling

  • Cancellations or reschedules with more than 24 hours notice: no charge.

Invoices are issued on completion of the shoot. Payment is due within 7 days unless a separate agency agreement is in place. We reserve the right to withhold final image files until payment is received.
