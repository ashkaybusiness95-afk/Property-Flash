Leeds photography FAQs

How much does property photography cost in Leeds?

From £80 for a full set of up to 25 edited, high-resolution images covering every room plus exteriors. Floor plans, drone shots and an EPC can be added to the same visit.

How quickly do I get the photos?

Same or next day. Most Leeds agents have the edited set in their inbox the evening of the shoot, ready to upload to Rightmove and Zoopla.

Do you photograph tenanted and HMO properties?

Yes — I manage HMOs of my own, so I'm comfortable working around tenants, arranging access and shooting rooms individually where needed.

Which parts of Leeds do you cover?

All of it. Regular shoots in Horsforth, Headingley, Chapel Allerton, Roundhay, Morley, Pudsey and the city centre, plus Otley and Wetherby.
