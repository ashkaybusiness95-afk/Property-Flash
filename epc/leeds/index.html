Leeds EPC FAQs

How long is an EPC valid?

An EPC is valid for 10 years from the date it is lodged on the national register. You can sell or let the property any number of times within that period using the same certificate.

What EPC rating do I need to rent out a property in Leeds?

The legal minimum for a rented property in England is currently band E. The government has proposed raising this to band C in the coming years, so if your Leeds rental is sitting at D or E it's worth planning improvements now. I can talk you through the recommendations on the certificate after the assessment.

How fast can you do an EPC in Leeds?

Usually same or next day. The visit takes around 30 minutes and the certificate is lodged on the national register within 24 hours, then emailed straight to you.

Do you cover all LS postcodes?

Yes — the whole Leeds district, LS1 through LS29, including Wetherby, Otley and Morley. If you're right on the edge of the district, message the postcode over WhatsApp and I'll confirm.
